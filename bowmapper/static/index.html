If the laser will not connect
1. Close sensorTOOL (only one program can own the port).
2. Still failing: open sensorTOOL, connect, disconnect, close it, then Connect here.
3. Check the COM port in Device Manager (FTDI / IF2001) and the 24 V supply.
4. Let the sensor warm up 20 minutes before measuring.

Die outline

NOT ALIGNED
Zero XY roughly at the die centre and set the standoff on the die. Then either enter the 4 corners by eye (fast), optionally Refine with laser (precise), or let Auto find do everything.

1 · Corners by eye

Jog the red spot onto each corner (0.1 / 0.01 steps near the corner), press = here. Or type work X/Y. Order does not matter.

2 · Laser

3 · Check the outline by eye

Drives the laser spot round the found die corners at the current Z. With Stop at corners it waits at each one until you press Resume, so you can look at where the red spot sits.

Height map of the die

Once the outline is verified: fills a grid inside it with the most accurate settings (raster, 400 ms settle, 200 samples, 3 µm noise limit with retries, drift check every 100 points). Check the estimate, Preview on map, then Start scan.
